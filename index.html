Run the whole shop from one screen.

A working prototype of our inventory, billing and ledger app. Sell something and watch stock, profit and ledgers update together.

Try it in three moves

  1. Tap items or press Scan on the Sell tab, then charge a sale.
  2. Open Home to see sales, profit and low-stock alerts update.
  3. Restock a low item and check the supplier ledger.

Prototype with sample data. Nothing is saved.
